hii40 is the Lexington standard H II region: plain gas on 13^3 cells, which runs in seconds.
hiipdr21 is the hiipdr benchmark, an H II region and its PDR, on a 21^3 grid with 2x10^6 packets and at most 7 iterations, which runs in about 10 minutes.
The steps to run both with the 3D PDR build and to plot them with plot_hii40.py and plot_hiipdr21.py are on the site's Quickstart page: https://mocassin-pdr.readthedocs.io/en/latest/getting-started/quickstart.html
