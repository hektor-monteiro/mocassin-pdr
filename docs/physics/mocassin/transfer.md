(sec:moc-transfer)=
# Monte Carlo transfer: sources, packets and estimators

MOCASSIN transports radiation as energy packets of constant energy, in the scheme of {cite:t}`Lucy1999` as adapted by {cite:t}`Ercolano2003` and extended to dust by {cite:t}`Ercolano2005`. {doc}`../pdr/field` already describes the core of it from the point of view of the FUV field: how a packet's frequency is drawn, the interaction optical depth, Henyey–Greenstein scattering, the split of an absorption between dust and gas, the optional biasing of {cite:t}`Baes2016`, and the path-length estimator of the mean intensity, Eq. ({eq}`eq:estimator`). This page describes the rest of the machinery, at every frequency, without repeating those. Units are those of the code: luminosities in $10^{36}$ erg s$^{-1}$, volumes in $10^{45}$ cm$^3$, lengths in cm.

(sec:moc-sources)=
## Sources and packet energies
{bdg-success}`used`

A single source of luminosity $L_\star$ emits $N$ packets per iteration (`nPhotons`), each of energy rate $\Delta E=L_\star/N$ (`deltaE`, {src}`code <continuum_mod.f90::setContinuum@"if (LPhot > 0. ) then ! calculate Lstar [e36 erg/s]">`). The luminosity is given directly (`LStar`) or through the hydrogen-ionising photon rate $Q$ (`LPhot`, $10^{36}$ s$^{-1}$), which fixes the radius of a star whose surface emits the source spectrum, $R_\star^2=Q/(4\pi\,N_{\mathrm{phot}})$ with $N_{\mathrm{phot}}$ the spectrum's photon flux above 1 Ryd, and then $L_\star=4\pi R_\star^2\sigma T_\star^4$ ({src}`continuum_mod.f90::setContinuum@"RStar = sqrt(Lphot / (fourPi*normConstantPhot*cRyd))"`). `starPosition` is given in units of the outermost axis value of each axis (default the origin, {src}`grid_mod.f90::setStarPosition@"starPosition(i)%x = starPosition(i)%x*xA(nxA)"`); packets start at that exact point, from the cell whose axis point is nearest. The source may sit in an inactive cell.

{bdg-warning}`not exercised` `multiPhotoSources` *file* reads, after the number of sources, one line per source with $T_\star$, $L_\star$, the spectrum, the position, a spectrum type and a time step. Every source gets $N_{\mathrm{tot}}/N_{\mathrm{src}}$ packets, so the packet energy $L_i/N_i$ differs from source to source ({src}`set_input_mod.f90::setMultiPhotoSources@"nPhotons(i) = NphotonsTot/nStars"`), and the directions are not folded into the `symmetricXYZ` octant. No model of this project has more than one source (the upstream example `examples/multistars.tar.gz` has not been run).

{bdg-warning}`not exercised` `diffuseSource` $L\,T\,$*shape*$\,N\,g$ adds a source distributed over the cells of the first $g$ grids (all grids if $g=0$). `setLdiffuse` shares $L$ among the active cells in proportion to $n_{\mathrm H}V$, or to $N_{\mathrm{dust}}V$ in a model without gas ({src}`continuum_mod.f90::setLdiffuse`), with $V$ from `getVolumeCon`, whose outer cells keep a full spacing even under `symmetricXYZ`, unlike the transport's volumes ({src}`continuum_mod.f90::getVolumeCon`, {lim}`L-74`); each cell emits $N/N_{\mathrm{cells}}$ packets per iteration ({src}`code <grid_mod.f90::fillGrid@"nPhotonsDiffuseLoc = nPhotonsDiffuse/totCellsloc">`), and the packet energy is set at each cell's call to its share divided by that number ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver@"deltaE(0) = grid(gpLoc)%LdiffuseLoc(grid(gpLoc)%active(cellLoc(1),cellLoc(2),cellLoc(3)))/NphotonsDiffuseLoc">`). These packets leave the cell's axis point in a random direction ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/newPhotonPacket@"case (\"diffExt\")">`) and are not stellar, so a PDR run counts them in the diffuse part of the field ({ref}`sec:split`).

(sec:moc-spectra)=
## Source spectra and frequency draws
{bdg-success}`used`

`contShape` names the spectrum. A name that opens as a file is read as a stellar atmosphere; otherwise it is `blackbody` or `powerlaw` ({src}`continuum_mod.f90::getFlux`). The blackbody is $B_\nu(T_\star)/h$, with the Wien form above $h\nu/kT=86$; the power law is $\nu^{-\alpha}$ between `nuMin` and `nuMax`, and cannot be normalised with `LPhot`. A file is read as two columns, wavelength in Å and $F_\lambda$, in the format of Rauch's model atmospheres {cite:p}`Rauch2003` ({src}`code <continuum_mod.f90::setContinuum@"case ('rauch')"+30>`); {bdg-warning}`not exercised` a source of type `sb99` in a `multiPhotoSources` file instead reads the spectrum of a {cite:t}`Leitherer1999` Starburst99 file at the source's time step ({src}`code <continuum_mod.f90::setContinuum@"case ('sb99')"+20>`). A tabulated spectrum is averaged over the points that fall in each bin. All the benchmarks of this project use blackbodies. `setProbDen` turns the spectrum into the cumulative distribution of emitted energy over the bins ({src}`continuum_mod.f90::setProbDen`),

$$
P_k=\frac{\sum_{i\le k}F_i\,\Delta\nu_i}{\sum_{i}F_i\,\Delta\nu_i},
$$ (eq:moc-cumul)

with the top value set to exactly 1; an energy rather than a photon distribution, since every packet carries the same energy. `getNu2` draws a uniform $\mathcal R$ and returns by bisection the bin $k+1$ above the last $k$ with $P_k\le\mathcal R$, so bin $k$ is drawn with probability $F_k\Delta\nu_k/\sum_iF_i\Delta\nu_i$ ({src}`photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/getNu2`); bin 1 is never returned, its share going to bin 2, nor is the top bin ({lim}`L-87`). The same routine draws from the per-cell continuum distributions below, `recPDF` and `dustPDF`; lines are drawn by `getLineP`.

(sec:moc-emission-pdfs)=
## New packets from the gas and the dust
{bdg-success}`used`

Every packet is created by `newPhotonPacket` ({src}`photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/newPhotonPacket`): at a source, at a diffuse-source cell, or at the point where the gas or the dust has absorbed a packet. Each cell's emission distributions are rebuilt at the start of every iteration by `emissionDriver`, from the ionisation, gas temperature and grain temperatures the previous iteration left ({doc}`emission`, {doc}`dust`). A packet re-emitted by the gas is a line with probability `totalLines`, the fraction of the cell's gas emission in the lines, and otherwise a continuum packet drawn from `recPDF`, the hydrogen and helium diffuse continua ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/newPhotonPacket@"call getNu2(grid(gP)%recPDF(grid(gP)%active(xP(igPn),yP(igPn),zP(igPn)),1:nbins), nuP)">`; {lim}`L-51`). A packet absorbed by dust is re-emitted from the cell's thermal dust spectrum, `dustPDF` ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/newPhotonPacket@"call getNu2(grid(gP)%dustPDF(grid(gP)%active(xP(igPn),yP(igPn),zP(igPn)),1:nbins), nuP)">`); since {devlog}`D-233` the two spectra are separate, and `recPDF` holds the gas alone.

A line is drawn from the cell's cumulative line distribution `linePDF`, which holds only the lines that fall in a bin of the frequency grid, with one slot for Ly$\alpha$ ({src}`photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/getLineP`, {src}`emission_mod.f90::setLineNuP`; {devlog}`D-296`). Since {devlog}`D-292` the line is transported as an ordinary packet in the bin that contains it (the default since {devlog}`D-307`, the only behaviour since {devlog}`D-352`); before, every line packet was dropped. A draw that lands on the lines with no bin still drops the packet ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/newPhotonPacket@"! D-292: with a frequency the packet is a normal diffuse"+8>`). In a model with dust and gas, a resonance line of `data/resLines.dat` (Ly$\alpha$ among them) is destroyed by the local grains with probability $1-f_{\mathrm{esc}}$, its escape probability in the cell, and its energy leaves as a dust-emitted packet ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/newPhotonPacket@"! D-376: a resonance line (data/resLines.dat, Lya among"..@"lgResDust = .true.">`; {devlog}`D-376`; {doc}`emission`). Every new packet leaves in an isotropic direction with no zero component ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/initPhotonPacket@"initPhotonPacket%direction = randomUnitVector()">`). Under `symmetricXYZ` only the packets of a single source are folded into the octant; re-emitted and diffuse-source packets keep their isotropic direction, and the mirror planes keep them in the octant ({ref}`sec:moc-symmetric`).

(sec:moc-path)=
## The walk through the cells
{bdg-success}`used`

`pathSegment` follows a packet from its creation to its absorption or escape ({src}`photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment`). It draws the optical depth $\tau$ to the next interaction ({src}`photon_mod.f90::calculate_pass_prob_and_weight`, {ref}`sec:field`), then steps from wall to wall. Each step runs to the nearest of the three walls of the current cell along the direction of flight, the walls lying midway between axis points ({ref}`sec:moc-cells`); its optical depth is $\kappa_\nu\,\Delta s$, with $\kappa_\nu$ the cell's total opacity at the packet's bin ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"tauCell = dS*grid(gP)%opacity(grid(gP)%active(xP,yP,zP), enPacket%nuP)">`). The packet interacts in the first active cell where the accumulated depth would pass $\tau$, at the distance

$$
\ell=\frac{\tau-\sum_{\mathrm{previous\ steps}}\kappa\,\Delta s}{\kappa_\nu}
$$ (eq:moc-dl)

into the step ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"dlLoc = (passProb-absTau)/grid(gP)%opacity(grid(gP)%active(xP,yP,zP), enPacket%nuP)">`). Both a full step and the partial one add $w\,\Delta E\,\ell/V$ to the cell's estimator ({ref}`sec:moc-estimators`). An inactive cell is crossed without interaction. The packet escapes if its interaction point lies at $r\ge R_{\mathrm{out}}>0$, after its last deposit ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"! check if the position within the cell is still within the outer radius"+2>`), or when it crosses the outer wall of an edge cell; a flight longer than `safeLimit` steps (50 000, or 5000 under plane-parallel illumination) is abandoned with a warning ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"safeLimit=50000">`).

The opacity is rebuilt at the start of every iteration from the previous iteration's state: the gas part by `ionizationDriver` from the ionisation fractions ({doc}`ionisation`), the dust part from each species and size whose temperature lies below its sublimation temperature ({src}`code <iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"if (grid(iG)%Tdust(nS,ai,grid(iG)%active(i,j,k))<TdustSublime(dcp-1+nS)) then">`; {doc}`dust`). A forced-skip scheme that pushed packets through an optically thick first cell with weight $e^{-\tau}$ was removed after it was found to underflow to zero weight above $\tau\approx87$ and delete the packets ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"! D-147 (removed). A forced-skip variance reduction lived here:">`; {devlog}`D-146`, {devlog}`D-147`).

(sec:moc-interaction)=
## Scattering, absorption and re-emission
{bdg-success}`used`

With dust, an interaction is a scattering with probability $\kappa^{\mathrm{sca}}_\nu/\kappa_\nu$; an absorption is given to the dust with probability $\kappa^{\mathrm{abs}}_\nu/(\kappa_\nu-\kappa^{\mathrm{sca}}_\nu)$ and to the gas otherwise ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"! Split by the absorption opacities themselves, which is"..@"! packet is absorbed by the gas">`; {devlog}`D-233`). Without dust every interaction is an absorption by the gas, since the gas opacity has no scattering term ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"! packet interacts with gas"+1>`).

Scattering is coherent. The new direction is drawn from the {cite:t}`HenyeyGreenstein1941` phase function about the incoming one ({src}`photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/hg`),

$$
\cos\theta=\frac{1}{2g}\left[1+g^2-\left(\frac{1-g^2}{1+g\,s}\right)^2\right],\qquad s=2\mathcal R-1,
$$ (eq:moc-hg)

isotropic when $g<10^{-4}$, with the azimuth uniform. The asymmetry parameter $g_\nu$ is one array over frequency for the whole model, the mean of the grains' $g$ weighted by their scattering cross-sections, $\langle g\rangle=\sum g\,C_{\mathrm{sca}}n/\sum C_{\mathrm{sca}}n$ ({src}`code <ph_mod.f90::makeDustXsec@"! D-280: the mean scattering cosine of a mixture is"..@"gSca(i)=gSca(i)/norm(i)">`); {devlog}`D-280` replaced the weighting by the geometric area, which had given `hiipdr`'s FUV $g=0.44$ instead of 0.61. With several dust components the array holds the last component's $g$ ({lim}`L-72`), and a direction that rounding pushes outside the unit sphere is renormalised but not stored ({lim}`L-73`). {bdg-success}`used` `isotropicScattering` (in the `-iso` runs of the 1D dust benchmark) draws an isotropic direction instead; {bdg-warning}`not exercised` `noScattering` sets the scattering efficiency to zero where `getQs` computes it ({src}`code <ph_mod.f90::getQs@"if (.not.lgDustScattering) Qsca(ai,i)=0.">`). A scattered packet is re-created as non-stellar ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"enPacket = initPhotonPacket(enPacket%nuP, rVec, enPacket%direction, .false., .false., enPacket%xP(1:2), &">`), so scattered starlight belongs to the diffuse field, and draws a new interaction depth.

An absorbed packet ends its flight: `pathSegment` hands its position, cell and absorber back with `reRun` = 1 ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"! the energy packet has beenid absorbed - reemit a new packet from this position"..@"reRun = 1">`), and `energyPacketDriver` calls `energyPacketRun` again, which emits a packet of the same energy and weight from the local gas or dust distribution ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/energyPacketRun@"if (reRun == 1) then">`). The weight is carried over from the routine's previous call in a local variable without SAVE, which works but is formally undefined; it differs from 1 only with `pathStretch` ({lim}`L-86`). A source packet is followed through at most `recursionLimit` = 5000 re-emissions; one that reaches the limit is counted as trapped ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver@"do i = 1, recursionLimit"#1>`). Energy is thus conserved packet by packet.

(sec:moc-subedge)=
## Gas-only models below the first edge
{bdg-success}`used`

In a model without dust, a packet created below the lowest ionisation threshold of the elements present, `ionEdge(1)`, is booked as escaped at once, in the bin and direction it was created with, and never transported ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/energyPacketRun@"if (.not.lgDust .and. enPacket%nu < ionEdge(1) .and. .not.enPacket%lgLine) then"+3>`). Below that threshold the gas has no bound–free opacity, and its free–free opacity is computed in the first bin only ({lim}`L-69`), so the short-cut leaves the ionisation balance and the emergent spectrum as they would be; the Lexington models run this way. But $J^{\mathrm{ste}}$ then holds no field below the edge: a gas-only model has no FUV field ({devlog}`D-019`). `writeFUVField` warns ({src}`code <output_mod.f90::writeFUVField@"if (.not.lgDust) then"#1>`) and a PDR run without dust stops at start-up ({src}`code <set_input_mod.f90::readInput@"if (lgPDR .and. .not.lgDust) then">`).

(sec:moc-pathstretch)=
## Path-length biasing
{bdg-warning}`not exercised`

`pathStretch` $\xi$, with $\xi\in(0,1]$, switches on the composite biasing of {cite:t}`Baes2016` ({devlog}`D-238`): the interaction depth is drawn from $q(\tau)=\xi e^{-\tau}+(1-\xi)\,\alpha e^{-\alpha\tau}$ and the packet's weight multiplied by

$$
w=\frac{e^{-\tau}}{q(\tau)}=\frac{1}{\xi+(1-\xi)\,\alpha\,e^{(1-\alpha)\tau}},\qquad \alpha=\frac{1}{1+R_{\mathrm{out}}\kappa_\nu},
$$ (eq:moc-baes)

with $\kappa_\nu$ the opacity of the cell where the flight starts, so that, for a uniform opacity, the stretched branch reaches the far side of the model ({src}`photon_mod.f90::calculate_pass_prob_and_weight`). The depth scale is recomputed for every flight after a scattering ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"tauPath = R_out*grid(gP)%opacity(grid(gP)%active(xP,yP,zP), enPacket%nuP)">`; {devlog}`D-239`), and a packet whose weight falls below $10^{-3}$ is killed or promoted back to $10^{-3}$ by Russian roulette ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/pathSegment@"if (lgPathStretch .and. enPacket%weight < wRoulette239) then"#1>`). The weight of one draw is bounded by $1/\xi$ but the product over a packet's flights is not; the radiated energy is unbiased with an eightfold larger run-to-run variance ({devlog}`D-240`, {devlog}`D-242`). The scheme is off by default, and no input of this project sets it. It was tested on `hiipdr` at {devlog}`D-238`–{devlog}`D-242` and {devlog}`D-280`: without it the C I-ionising field was exactly zero in 63–100% of the cells beyond $A_V=2.3$, with it in at most 9% to $A_V=2.8$. Under plane-parallel illumination $R_{\mathrm{out}}$ is not a length and $\alpha$ would vanish ({lim}`L-74`).

(sec:moc-estimators)=
## The field estimators
{bdg-success}`used`

Every step of every packet adds $w\,\Delta E\,\ell/V$ to $J^{\mathrm{ste}}$ of its cell and bin, Eq. ({eq}`eq:estimator`), for stellar and non-stellar packets alike; the non-stellar part is mirrored into $J^{\mathrm{dif}}$ in a PDR run, and goes to $J^{\mathrm{dif}}$ alone in debug mode ({ref}`sec:split`). Each rank accumulates its own packets' deposits over the whole grid; the arrays are then summed over ranks ({src}`code <iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"call mpi_allreduce(grid(iG)%JSte, JSteTemp, size, &">`), multiplied by $10^{-9}$ to reach erg s$^{-1}$ cm$^{-2}$ and divided by 8 under `symmetricXYZ` ({numref}`tab:moc-iter`). $J^{\mathrm{ste}}_k$ is already an integral over bin $k$: the photoionisation rates sum $J^{\mathrm{ste}}_k\sigma_k/h\nu_k$ over the bins with no $\Delta\nu_k$ (Eq. {eq}`eq:gammaX`; {doc}`ionisation`), as do the heating integrals ({doc}`thermal`) and the grain energy balance ({doc}`dust`), while a mean intensity per unit frequency is $J^{\mathrm{ste}}_k/(4\pi\Delta\nu_k)$ ({ref}`sec:eta`).

(sec:moc-sed)=
## Escaping packets and the SED
{bdg-success}`used`

Every escape adds $w\,\Delta E$ to `escapedPackets`(cell, bin, view) of the cell where the packet was last emitted or scattered (`origin`, {src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/initPhotonPacket@"initPhotonPacket%origin(2) = grid(gP)%active(initPhotonPacket%xP(igpi),&">`). View 0 takes every escape. For the viewpoints the direction is binned in $\theta=\arccos d_z$ (20 bins over $\pi$) and $\phi=\arctan(d_y/d_x)$ (40 bins over $2\pi$; {src}`code <common_mod.f90@"integer :: TotAngleBinsTheta=20 ! total # of theta angle bins for SED"+1>`), with $|d_z|$ and $|d_y|/|d_x|$ under `symmetricXYZ`. `inclination` $n\,\theta_1\,\phi_1\dots$ defines at most two viewpoints (radians), used by `test_Problem` and the 2D dust disc inputs. A viewpoint collects the packets of its $\theta$ bin that are also in its $\phi$ bin; a negative $\phi$ turns the $\phi$ dependence off, and since the test also accepts a match between the viewpoint's $\phi$ and that of an empty slot (zero), a viewpoint at $\phi=0$, as in `test_Problem`, collects its whole $\theta$ bin as well ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/energyPacketRun@"elseif (viewPointPtheta(idirT) == viewPointPphi(idirP).or. &">`). Under `symmetricXYZ` the viewpoints are scaled by 4 where the angle average is scaled by 8, which with the far-infrared normalisation below puts a viewpoint's far infrared at half the average, read from the code and not checked by a run ({lim}`L-80`). The $\phi$ bin uses `atan` rather than `atan2`, so without `symmetricXYZ` directions with $d_x<0$ land in the opposite bin ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/energyPacketRun@"idirP = int(atan(enPacket%direction%y/enPacket%direction%x)/dPhi)">`; {lim}`L-74`).

`writeSED` writes `output/SED.out` at every iteration ({src}`output_mod.f90::writeSED`). It sums the escaped energy over all origin cells, including slot 0, the packets from an inactive source cell ({src}`code <output_mod.f90::writeSED@"! D-441: bucket 0 holds the packets whose origin cell is inactive,">`; before {devlog}`D-441` they were left out, 40% of `LStar` for the quickstart's HII40), and converts it to $F_\nu D^2$ in Jy pc$^2$:

$$
F_\nu D^2=\frac{10^{23}}{4\pi\,(1\,\mathrm{pc})^2}\,\frac{E_k}{\Delta\nu_k}
$$ (eq:moc-sed)

with $E_k$ the escaped energy rate in bin $k$ (multiplied by 8 under `symmetricXYZ`) and $\Delta\nu_k$ in Hz. A viewpoint's spectrum replaces $4\pi$ by $4\pi\,r_v$, where $r_v$ is the share of the far-infrared luminosity ($\lambda\ge150$ µm) escaping in that viewpoint's bins ({src}`code <output_mod.f90::writeSED@"SEDratio = SUM(Luminosity(1:farIR_ind(1), imu))/SUM(Luminosity(1:farIR_ind(1), 0))">`), so that its far infrared matches the angle average; $r_v$ is set to 1 before the loop, which had read it uninitialised with no viewpoint ({devlog}`D-343`). A second set of columns holds the packets that escaped from the source cells only; {bdg-warning}`not exercised` `NoSourceSED` leaves those out of the first set, and `echo` weights each cell by its light-echo volume. The trailer gives the escaped luminosity in $10^{36}$ erg s$^{-1}$ ({devlog}`D-441`), which equals `LStar` to $10^{-4}$ in every dust run of that entry; since {devlog}`D-454` two lines follow, the escaped lines' luminosity and the sum of the two.

A transported line ({ref}`sec:moc-emission-pdfs`) is flagged `lgLineTr` when it is created ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver/newPhotonPacket@"if (.not.lgResDust) newPhotonPacket%lgLineTr = .true.">`) and keeps the flag through dust scattering. A resonance line the grains destroy is re-emitted as dust emission, which is continuum, and is not flagged. A flagged packet's escape adds to `escapedLines`(bin), over all directions, instead of `escapedPackets` ({devlog}`D-454`), so `SED.out` is the continuum, as it was before {devlog}`D-292`, and so is `contCube.out`. `writeSED` writes the lines to `SEDlines.out` on the same grid and in the same units ({src}`code <output_mod.f90::writeSED@"file='output/SEDlines.out'">`). They are raw sums: under `symmetricXYZ` they take neither the $/8$ of `escapedPackets` nor the $\times8$ of Eq. ({eq}`eq:moc-sed`), which cancel. There are no viewpoint or source columns, and `echo` and `NoSourceSED` do not apply. The two files together reproduce the `SED.out` of the code before: on `hiipdr` the trailers add up to the earlier total within $2\times10^{-7}$, with the lines 0.043 of it, and on the dust-free HII40 the lines are 0.275 of it, more than half of that Ly$\alpha$ ({devlog}`D-454`).

(sec:moc-seed)=
## Random numbers
{bdg-success}`used`

`energyPacketDriver` re-seeds the generator at each of its calls, once per source per iteration and once per cell for a diffuse source ({src}`code <photon_mod.f90::energyPacketDriver@"! D-334: seed the generator. This routine is called once per star per"..@"call random_seed(put = seed)">`). With the default `randomSeed` $-1$ the running state is perturbed by the clock's milliseconds and the rank number, and no run can be repeated. With `randomSeed` $\ge0$ the state is replaced by an xorshift mix of the seed, the rank and the number of calls the rank has made, so a run is repeated exactly by the same build at the same rank count ({devlog}`D-334`); the quickstart and tutorial examples set it. `fillingFactor`'s draws fall outside it ({lim}`L-74`).

(sec:moc-mpi)=
## Division of the work between ranks
{bdg-success}`used`

Every rank holds the whole grid. A source's $N$ packets are dealt as $N/N_{\mathrm{ranks}}$ per rank, the remainder going one each to the lowest ranks ({src}`code <iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"load = nPhotons(iStar)/numtasks">`), and each rank follows its packets through the whole grid; a diffuse source's packets are dealt in the same way cell by cell. The cell work (`ionizationDriver`, `emissionDriver`, `updateCell`) is dealt round-robin: a rank takes the cells whose running number satisfies $\mathrm{mod}(i_{\mathrm{cell}}-r-1,\,N_{\mathrm{ranks}})=0$ for its rank $r$ ({src}`code <iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"if (mod(iCell-(taskid+1),numtasks)==0) &"#1>`). Each product is then summed over ranks with `MPI_Allreduce`, every rank holding zeros in the cells it did not own: the opacity; `recPDF`, `dustPDF`, `linePDF` and `totalLines`; the escaped packets (in blocks of at most $10^6$ cells, to keep the count within 32 bits); $J^{\mathrm{ste}}$ and $J^{\mathrm{dif}}$; the plane-parallel launch counts; and after `updateCell` the temperatures, electron densities, ionisation fractions, grain temperatures and convergence flags ({numref}`tab:moc-iter`). The result depends on the rank count through the random streams and through the order in which `MPI_Allreduce` adds the ranks' contributions, which changes the rounding of every sum.
