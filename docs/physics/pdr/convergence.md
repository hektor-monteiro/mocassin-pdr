(sec:convergence)=
# Iteration control and convergence
## The fixed point

The model is converged when the field, the ionisation, the temperatures, the molecular abundances and the H$_2$ level populations reproduce one another from one MC iteration to the next. Because the columns and the level populations lag by one iteration ({ref}`sec:lags`) and the level model makes one sweep per iteration, the shielding and the populations converge together with the rest of the model rather than inside each iteration. This is a Picard (successive-substitution) iteration on the coupled system; there is no acceleration of the outer iteration. Damping across iterations (`iterDamp`, {src}`iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"if (lgPDR .and. iterDamp > 0. .and. nIterateMC >= pdrStartIter) then"..@"if (nIterateMC >= pdrStartIter) call pdrToMocassin(grid(1:nGrids))"`) is off by default ({devlog}`D-406`). Because the MC field is redrawn at every iteration, the fixed point is reached only to within the MC noise of {ref}`sec:mcnoise`.

- **Integrated quantities** ({src}`common_mod.f90@"! ---------------------------------------------------------------- D-391"`, test at {src}`iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"if ( lgCvTotals .and. (sec:mcnoise)=
## Monte Carlo noise
Since every photorate reads its own cell’s MC field ({devlog}`D-350`), the chemistry of a cell inherits the cell’s sampling noise, redrawn at every iteration because $J$ is rebuilt from zero. On `hiipdr` at $6\times10^6$ packets the seed-to-seed scatter of the FUV field per cell is 2–4% at $A_{V}\sim1$, 7% at 1.6–2.1, 14% at 2.1–2.6 and 22% (90th percentile 38%) at 2.6–3.05 ({lim}`L-26`). It follows $N^{-1/2}$ in the number of packet tracks per cell: on a $61^3$ grid with $5\times10^8$ packets, 21 times the tracks per cell, the iteration-to-iteration noise is 0.8, 1.5, 2.8 and 4.8% at $A_{V}=1$–1.5, 1.5–2, 2–2.5 and 2.5–3.5 ({devlog}`D-365`). For the level model the noise is that of a single 2%-wide frequency bin per line rather than of the band: its per-cell Solomon rate scatters about 1.5 times as much as the chemistry’s band-integrated rate on the same field, and moved by 50–60% (median) between iterations against 3.4% on a fixed field ({devlog}`H-012`).

The noise has three consequences beyond scatter. Through the non-linear dependence of the photorates on the field it biases cell means, by an estimated $+5\%$ at 22% scatter ({lim}`L-26`), and the bias of the means is one way: fewer packets give a shallower carbon turn and colder deep gas on `hiipdr` ({devlog}`D-408`). At the carbon turn the noise is not scatter about a mean but two states: 85–97% of the cells in each 0.1 mag bin between $A_{V}=1.9$ and 2.6 are either ionised in carbon or in the state maintained by cosmic rays alone, depending on whether a packet of the 11.26–13.6 eV band crossed them in that iteration, so “C$^+=$C” marks where half the cells are missed ({devlog}`D-405`). And the noise is redrawn in full at every iteration: the spread of a cell’s mean over seven to nine iterations among symmetry-equivalent cells is 0.33–0.41 of one iteration’s ({lim}`L-26`). An estimator accumulated over iterations is registered for future development ({devlog}`D-411`).

What converges at the default $6\times10^6$ packets was measured by quadrupling the count three times ({devlog}`D-408`, {devlog}`H-032`). The H$_2$ lines, the ionised gas’s lines, \[O I\], H$\beta$ and the H/H$_2$ crossing are converged at the default ($A_{V}=1.395$ at $6\times10^6$ and 1.394 at $3.84\times10^8$ packets on `hiipdr`; 1–0 S(1) changes by 0.8%). The carbon turn and the \[C I\] lines need 16–64 times the default, and CO still moves at 64 times (by 5–10% per factor of four on `hiipdr`). Per-cell values of the deep chemistry are not converged at any of these counts.

## End of the run

When the stop test passes, or at the maximum iteration count, the code keeps the second ionisation solve (`ionKeepSecond`), calls `h2LevelModel(grid,12)` ({src}`iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"! runs on the stages that are written."`, {src}`iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"if (lgPDR) call h2LevelModel(grid(1:nGrids), 12) ! H-007, H-012"`) and writes the outputs; the chemistry is not solved again on the final level populations ({ref}`sec:limitations`). The level model’s outputs are written by {src}`h2Lines_mod.f90`: `output/lineFluxH2.out` (every H$_2$ $X$-state line above $10^{-6}$ of the strongest, integrated over the grid, optically thin, no extinction), `output/h2Cells.out` (per cell: $n(\mathrm{H_2})$, $T_e$, $k_{\mathrm{Sol}}$ and the chemistry’s own rate on the same field, pumping rate, ortho/para, fraction above 4100 cm$^{-1}$, the H$_2$ heating terms beside 3D-PDR’s and MOCASSIN’s heating and cooling) and `output/h2Pop.bin` (all populations, read back by a warm start).
