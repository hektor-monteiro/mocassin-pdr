(sec:feedback)=
# Feedback from the molecules on MOCASSIN
The molecular content computed in {ref}`sec:chemistry` acts back on the radiation field, the ionisation and the temperature, which closes the loop of {numref}`fig:chain`. It does so through three channels only: the fraction of each element held in molecules, the H$_2$ photoionisation continuum, and the heating and cooling terms. No molecular line enters the MC transfer.

## Elements locked in molecules

The whole handback for an element is the fraction of it *not* in molecules (`setElemAvail`, {src}`pdr_mod.f90::setElemAvail`, called by `pdrToMocassin`, {src}`pdr_mod.f90::pdrToMocassin`): 

```{math}
`elemAvail`_X = \min\!\Bigl[\max\Bigl(1-\frac{M_X}{A_X},\,0\Bigr),\,1\Bigr],
  \qquad M_X = \sum_i \nu_{X,i}\,x_i ,
```

 for the five linked elements H, He, C, O and Mg, with $A_X$ MOCASSIN’s own abundance ($A_{\mathrm H}=1$) and $\nu_{X,i}$ the number of atoms of $X$ in species $i$, read from the species’ formula for every species other than $X$ and $X^+$ themselves ({src}`pdr_mod.f90::initPDRnetwork@"if (allocated(pdrMolAtoms)) deallocate(pdrMolAtoms)"..@"pdrMolAtoms(iS, iLnk) = dble(nAt402(pdrElZ(iLnk)))"`, {devlog}`D-402`). MOCASSIN’s `ionBalance` constrains the sum of the element’s ion stages to it ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/ionBalance@"availFrac(elem) = &"`): carbon in CO therefore appears in no atomic stage, emits no atomic line and gives no free electron. The chemistry writes no ionisation state and no temperature ({devlog}`D-193`). After each iteration `rescaleIonToAvail` ({src}`pdr_mod.f90::rescaleIonToAvail`) rescales the stages of every element to `elemAvail`, keeping MOCASSIN’s ratios between them, so that the hydrogen budget $x(\mathrm H^0)+x(\mathrm H^+)+2x(\mathrm{H_2})=1$ holds in every cell (up to the hydrogen in other molecules) before the next transfer builds its opacity ({devlog}`D-220`). It prints the size of the pre-rescale defect (`[D220]`), which is the informative test of the budget: since the kept second solve (below) no cell of either fixture is off by more than 1% in the written model ({lim}`L-29`).

The network channels classified in {ref}`sec:chemistry` (sources and sinks of $X$ and $X^+$ to and from molecules, and net conversions between them) enter `ionBalance`’s rate matrix as sinks on the diagonal and sources on the right-hand side, the sum still constrained to `elemAvail` ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/ionBalance@"! is left entirely to the ion solver."..@"! chemical terms on the same scaled system: the sink is a"`, {devlog}`D-402`). They are those of the previous chemistry, i.e. one iteration behind ({lim}`L-47`). Cosmic-ray ionisation, which MOCASSIN otherwise lacks, is added as MOCASSIN’s own physics with the network’s coefficients: $\alpha\,\zeta/\zeta_0$ for direct ionisation and $\alpha\,(\zeta/\zeta_0)(T/300\,\mathrm K)^\beta\gamma/(1-\omega)$ for the cosmic-ray-induced photons, $\zeta_0=1.3\times10^{-17}$ s$^{-1}$, for every linked neutral ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/ionBalance@"if (lgPDR .and. ion == 1 .and. pdrNcr > 0) then"..@"& * pdrCrGamma(iCr280)/(1.d0 - dble(omega280))"`; {devlog}`D-193`, {devlog}`D-280`).

## H$_2$ in the gas opacity

$n(\mathrm{H_2})$ (`H2den`) adds the H$_2$ photoionisation continuum above 15.4 eV to the gas opacity of the next transfer (`addOpacity`, {src}`ionization_mod.f90::addOpacity@"! D-107: H2 photoionization continuum, threshold 15.43 eV = 1.1345 Ryd."..@"opacity(i) = opacity(i) + H2density*yanH2xSec(nuArray(i))"`, {devlog}`D-107`), 

```{math}
\kappa_\nu \mathrel{+}= n(\mathrm{H_2})\,\sigma_{\mathrm{H_2}}(\nu),\qquad h\nu\ge15.4\,\mathrm{eV},
```

 with the cross section fitted by {cite:t}`Yan1998`, as revised in their erratum {cite:p}`Yan2001` (`yanH2xSec`, {src}`ionization_mod.f90::yanH2xSec`; zero below 15.4 eV, as in Cloudy, and clipped at zero just above threshold where the fit goes negative). The comment above the opacity loop ({src}`ionization_mod.f90::addOpacity@"! sigma(nu): threshold ~6e-18 cm^2 rising to a broad peak of order"..@"! this failure mode."`) still describes “a single power law anchored at threshold”; the function actually used is the piecewise fit of {cite:t}`Yan1998`. This continuum matters only for hydrogen-ionising photons that reach molecular gas. No H$_2$, CO or H I Lyman-series *line* opacity is added to the MC transfer: line absorption is represented only through the shielding columns.

(sec:thermal)=
## Heating and cooling
MOCASSIN solves the thermal balance of every cell, heating equal to cooling per H nucleus, with `thermBalance` ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/thermBalance`) inside its temperature search `iterateT` ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/iterateT`). Its own terms are the photoionisation heating by every ion on the MC field; the grain photoelectric heating (`setPhotoelHeatCool`, {src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/setPhotoelHeatCool`), summed over every grain species and size from the MC field above the photoelectric threshold (work function plus grain potential), with the yield of {cite:t}`WeingartnerDraine2001` ({devlog}`D-289`) and the grain charge of MOCASSIN’s own charge balance, whose electron sticking was set to the same paper’s value ({devlog}`D-326`); the recombination, free–free and collisionally excited line cooling of every ion; and the gas–grain collisional exchange (`setDustGasCollHeatCool`, {src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/setDustGasCollHeatCool`). The molecules enter it in four ways.

0.  **Gas–grain exchange with H$_2$.** Since {devlog}`D-420` H$_2$ is a collider in the exchange: a neutral of mass $m=2m_{\mathrm H}$ striking each grain species and size with the accommodation coefficient $2mM/(m+M)^2$ of the grain's surface atoms of mass $M$, as Cloudy takes it, at the chemistry's $n(\mathrm{H_2})$ ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/setDustGasCollHeatCool@"nH2coll = 0."..@"& grid%Tdust(ns,na,cellP))"`). Before, only atoms, ions and electrons collided, and the atoms' stages sum to the part of each element not in molecules, so in molecular gas almost nothing coupled the gas to the dust ({lim}`L-9`). {devlog}`D-421` put the term into each balance in that balance's own units: physical per H nucleus in the gas balance, and divided by $\pi$ in the grain balance, whose field is $J^{\mathrm{ste}}/\pi$ ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/setDustGasCollHeatCool@"gasDustColl_d(:,:)= gasDustColl_d(:,:)/Pi"`). The earlier division of both by $4\pi$ had left the gas term $4\pi$ too weak and the grain term 4 times too weak. In the v2.0 release runs the exchange carries 7.4% of `hiipdr`'s cooling and 1.5% of `pn150k`'s.

1.  **Coolants.** The coolants named by `pdrCoolants` (in the fixtures C$^+$, C, O and $^{12}$CO, LAMDA-format files, {cite:t}`Schoier2005`) are treated in two ways.

    - C$^+$, C and O are cooled by MOCASSIN’s own model atoms (its electron collision data and all its levels), to which the coolant files’ rates for collisions with H, H$^+$, He and H$_2$ and the chemistry’s escape probability of each transition are added ({src}`emission_mod.f90::equilibrium@"! D-363: ONE ATOM PER ION. For an ion the chemistry also carries (C I,"..@"#endif"`; {src}`common_mod.f90::buildPdrAtomMap`, {src}`common_mod.f90::setLineColliders`, {src}`common_mod.f90::pdrNeutralRates`; {devlog}`D-363`). In all of MOCASSIN's atoms the Boltzmann factor of a collisional rate goes with the excitation out of the lower-energy level of each pair, found from the level energies rather than the level index, since 31 data files list levels out of energy order ({src}`emission_mod.f90::equilibrium@"! D-418: the Boltzmann factor belongs to the LOWER level of the"..@"end if"`; {devlog}`D-418`). They are solved at every trial temperature, and the same atoms give the reported line fluxes, so the thermal balance and the lines share one atom per ion.

    - CO, which MOCASSIN has no atom for, is solved by 3D-PDR’s escape-probability level-population solver in `pdrStep` at 16 temperatures log-spaced over \[`pdrTmin`, `pdrTmax`\], a factor 1.94 apart for the defaults (`initPDRcoolTab`, {src}`pdr_mod.f90::initPDRcoolTab`, {devlog}`D-215`). The temperature search reads this table by log–log interpolation, clamped at its ends (`pdrCoolAt`, {src}`common_mod.f90::pdrCoolAt`), and adds it to MOCASSIN’s cooling ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/thermBalance@"pdrCoolMol = 0."..@"! molecules, which MOCASSIN has no atom for, are added."`). For CO the table misses an exact solve at the cell’s temperature by a mean $|\Delta\ln\Lambda|$ of 0.02 (`hiipdr`) and 0.004 (`pn150k`) per cell ({lim}`L-36`).

    In both the level populations are in statistical equilibrium with the colliders H, H$^+$, $e^-$, He and H$_2$ at the chemistry’s abundances (the linked ions and $n_e$ being MOCASSIN’s). The escape probability of a transition $u\to l$ is the mean over the twelve line rays of {ref}`sec:lineRays` of $\beta=(1-e^{-\tau})/\tau$, with 

```{math}
\tau = \frac{A_{ul}\,c^3}{8\pi\nu_{ul}^3\,b}\sum_{\mathrm{cells}}
               \Bigl(n_l\frac{g_u}{g_l}-n_u\Bigr)\ell ,
```

 each crossed cell contributing its own level populations as last stored ({src}`pdr/escape_probability.F90::escape_probability@"S_ij=TMP2/TPOP"+18`, {src}`pdr/escape_probability.F90::escape_probability@"beta_ij_sum=sum(beta_ij_ray)"..@"beta_ij = beta_ij_sum / real(nrays,kind=DP)"`; {devlog}`D-377`). With `-DSOBOLEV` the rate equations use the equivalent net-rate form, the spontaneous rate $A_{ul}\beta$ and the radiative rates driven by $\beta J_{\mathrm{bg}}$ ({src}`pdr/escape_probability.F90::escape_probability@"! Sobolev (ALI-like) net-rate form: because the source function obeys"..@"beta_save(jlevel,ilevel) = beta_ij"`), where the background $J_{\mathrm{bg}}$ is MOCASSIN’s own $J_\nu$ at the line plus the CMB ({devlog}`D-377`). The cooling is the net escaping power, $\Lambda_{ul}=A_{ul}h\nu_{ul}n_u\beta\,(S_{ul}-J_{\mathrm{bg}})/S_{ul}$ with $S_{ul}$ the line source function ({src}`pdr/escape_probability.F90::escape_probability@"if (S_ij.ne.0.0D0) then"`).

2.  **Extra heating.** 3D-PDR’s heating routine `CALC_HEATING` ({src}`pdr/heatingfunctions.F90`) is called at {src}`pdr_mod.f90::pdrStep@"call CALC_HEATING(nH, dble(grid%Te(cellP)), Td, chiLoc, dble(pdrVturb)*1.d5, &"+2` with the local field $\chi$ at MOCASSIN’s $T_e$. The terms MOCASSIN computes itself are excluded: grain and PAH photoelectric heating (3D-PDR’s {cite:t}`BakesTielens1994` expression with the modifications of {cite:t}`Wolfire2003`, and its alternative {cite:t}`WeingartnerDraine2001` fit), carbon photoionisation heating and gas–grain collisions; these are the only terms that read $\chi$. The rest, `pdrHeatXtra` ({src}`pdr_mod.f90::pdrStep@"grid%pdrHeatXtra(cellP) = real(heat(5) + heat(6) + heat(7) + &"`), is added to MOCASSIN’s heating ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/thermBalance@"heatInt = heatInt + grid%pdrHeatXtra(cellP)/&"+1`) and held fixed during the temperature search:

    - *H$_2$ terms from the level model* ({src}`pdr_mod.f90::pdrStep@"! ---------------------------------------------------------- H-013"..@"heat(12) = heat(2) + heat(4) + sum(heat(5:11))"`, {devlog}`H-013`): formation, $R_{\mathrm f}\,n_{\mathrm H}\,n(\mathrm H)\,E_{\mathrm{kin}}$, with $E_{\mathrm{kin}}$ the kinetic energy of a newly formed molecule from the formation distribution; dissociation, $n(\mathrm{H_2})\sum_l n_l H^{\mathrm{dis}}_l$, the kinetic energy of the atoms; and the net collisional term $n(\mathrm{H_2})\,\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{coll}}$, which is the heating by the de-excitation of pumped and newly formed molecules and, where the gas is warmer than the level populations, H$_2$’s line cooling ({ref}`sec:solvecell`). They replace 3D-PDR’s fixed energies (1.5 eV per formation, which the vendored code attributes to {cite:t}`HollenbachTielens1999`; 0.4 eV per dissociation; $9\times2.2$ eV per dissociation for pumping, with the critical-density factor it attributes to {cite:t}`HollenbachMcKee1979`), which are kept beside them for comparison (`h2Cells.out`);

    - cosmic-ray heating, $\Gamma_{\mathrm{CR}}=9.4\,\mathrm{eV}\times\zeta\,n(\mathrm{H_2})$, i.e. $1.5\times10^{-11}\zeta\,n(\mathrm{H_2})$ erg cm$^{-3}$ s$^{-1}$, the expression of {cite:t}`TielensHollenbach1985`, their eq. A23: about 8 eV of heat per primary ionisation in molecular gas, helium included, with $\zeta$ the rate per H$_2$. Ionisations in atomic gas deposit no heat in this term, which {cite:t}`TielensHollenbach1985` justify by cosmic-ray heating being unimportant there;

    - turbulent dissipation, $\Gamma_{\mathrm{turb}}=3.5\times10^{-28}\,(v_{\mathrm{turb}}/\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}})^3\,
          (L_{\mathrm{turb}}/\mathrm{pc})^{-1}\,n_{\mathrm H}$ erg cm$^{-3}$ s$^{-1}$ with $L_{\mathrm{turb}}=5$ pc fixed and $v_{\mathrm{turb}}$ = `pdrVturb`, which the vendored code attributes to {cite:t}`Black1987`: $7\times10^{-29}$ erg s$^{-1}$ per H nucleus at the default 1 km s$^{-1}$;

    - the energy released by exothermic reactions (for example the dissociative recombination of HCO$^+$, H$_3^+$ and H$_3$O$^+$, and He$^+$ with H$_2$ and CO), the reactions being found automatically in the network, after {cite:t}`Clavel1978`, who found that such reactions could contribute significantly to the heating if their energy is released entirely as kinetic energy, as is assumed here.

3.  **Photoelectric heating** is MOCASSIN’s own, from its grains, as described above ({devlog}`D-289`, {devlog}`D-326`); 3D-PDR’s PAH term is not used ({devlog}`D-136`, {devlog}`D-352`). The model grains include no PAHs ({lim}`L-7`), so the heating lacks the contribution of PAHs and very small grains that dominates in the {cite:t}`BakesTielens1994` prescription of most PDR codes and of the code comparison {cite:p}`Rollig2007`; the Cloudy reference models omit them too. Against Cloudy the photoelectric efficiency per H per unit field is 0.66–1.10 with no trend with depth on `pn150k` ({devlog}`D-304`); on `hiipdr` the gas temperature at $A_{V}=0.73$ and 0.97 is 1.07 and 1.01 times Cloudy’s since the sticking fix, the deeper gas remaining colder ({lim}`L-22`).

The gas temperature found this way is the one the next chemistry runs at, and, one iteration later, the level model. It acts on the molecules through the rate coefficients, through the H$_2$ formation coefficient (the thermal speed of the H atoms and their sticking probability, {ref}`sec:formation`), through the collisional excitation of H$_2$ and of the coolants, and through the CO cooling it controls. The dust temperature acts through the formation efficiency ({ref}`sec:Td`). These are channels by which the field sets the molecular content even where no FUV photon reaches the molecules.

## The second ionisation solve

After the chemistry, `ionBalance` is solved a second time in every cell, at the temperature of the first solve, on the channels and the molecular content this iteration’s chemistry has just left; it is counted against the first and the model is put back (`ionSecondSolve`, {src}`iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/ionSecondSolve`, called at {src}`iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"call ionSecondSolve(iG, 2)"`; {devlog}`D-404`). A control pass on the old terms, before the chemistry ({src}`iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"call ionSecondSolve(iG, 1)"`), moves nothing. On the finished model the second solve’s stages replace the first’s (`ionKeepSecond`, {src}`iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/ionKeepSecond`), so the written ions are consistent with the last chemistry, and the level model’s final call runs on them ({devlog}`H-029`). The second solve removes the one-iteration delay of {lim}`L-47`: the neutral cells whose solver $X^+/X$ is more than 10% from MOCASSIN’s fall from 28.4% to 0.0% for hydrogen on `hiipdr`, and to 0.0% for every linked element except oxygen there (9.7%, {lim}`L-50`). Neither the temperature nor the chemistry is solved again on the second solve’s ions, and in the iterations before the last the transfer, the thermal balance and the chemistry still run on the first solve’s.
