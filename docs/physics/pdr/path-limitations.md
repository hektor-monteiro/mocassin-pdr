(sec:limitations)=
# Known limitations and open issues
This section lists the limitations that bear on the path from the field to the molecules, with the direction of the bias each introduces and its measured size where one exists. The first group is recorded in the project’s own logs. The second group was found while reading the code for this document and is not, as far as could be found, recorded in `DEVLOG.md`, `DEVLOG-H2.md` or `doc/pdr-status.md`; its sizes have not been measured.

## Recorded in the project’s logs

1.  **Per-cell Monte Carlo noise in the photorates** ({lim}`L-26`, {devlog}`D-405`, {devlog}`H-012`). Every photorate reads its own cell’s field, whose per-cell scatter reaches 14–22% beyond $A_{V}\sim2$ at the default packet count; the level model’s per-line field is noisier than the band integral. Besides scatter, the noise biases cell means through the non-linear rates (estimated $+5\%$ at 22% scatter) and, at the carbon turn, decides which of two states a cell is in ({devlog}`D-405`). An estimator accumulated over iterations is registered for future development ({devlog}`D-411`). {ref}`sec:mcnoise` gives the numbers.

2.  **Packet convergence of carbon and CO** ({devlog}`D-408`, {devlog}`H-032`). At the default $6\times10^6$ packets the H$_2$ lines and the H/H$_2$ crossing are converged, but the carbon turn is placed too shallow (C$^+=$C moves by 0.5 mag on `hiipdr` up to $3.84\times10^8$ packets, towards Cloudy’s), the deep gas is too cold, \[C I\] needs 16–64 times the packets and CO is still moving at 64 times. The stop test does not see this.

3.  **The twelve-direction quadrature** ({lim}`L-53`, {devlog}`D-410`): cells that the grid’s symmetry makes equal differ in $n(\mathrm{H_2})$ by up to about 11% following the ring axis of the twelve directions, independently of the packet count; 48 directions remove the pattern at 1.48 times the run time, moving totals by under 1%. The twelve were kept by decision, since no line map carries the pattern (CO’s abundance and the H$_2$ density do).

4.  **Damping width of the H$_2$ lines** ({devlog}`H-008`, {devlog}`H-026`). The code damps each line with the upper level’s total radiative decay rate (to the bound levels and the continuum of $X$), which is the physical natural width; damping each line with its own $A$, as Cloudy does, narrows the wings, strengthens the shielding and moves the H/H$_2$ crossing 0.21 mag shallower on `hiipdr` (from $A_{V}=1.396$ to 1.188, Cloudy’s being 1.280) and 0.10 mag shallower on `pn150k`. Between $A_{V}=1.05$ and 1.2 on `hiipdr` the model’s photodissociation rate per molecule is 5–7 times Cloudy’s. On `hiipdr` the width accounts for $+0.21$ mag of the difference between the two codes’ crossings and everything else for $-0.09$ mag ({devlog}`H-026`); it is the largest single identified term and should accompany any comparison with Cloudy, which uses the total decay rate for every species but H$_2$ ({devlog}`H-008`).

5.  **No H I Lyman-series shading** of the H$_2$ band (`DEVLOG-H2.md`, {devlog}`H-006`, “What is not yet known”). Neither the MC transfer nor the level model contains it, so pumping and dissociation are overestimated where a large H I column lies in front of the molecular gas, and the transition is placed too deep. The hydrogen column between the ionisation front and the H/H$_2$ crossing is about $1.8\times10^{21}$ cm$^{-2}$ on `hiipdr` ($A_{V}$ 0.48 to 1.40 at $1.93\times10^{21}$ cm$^{-2}$ mag$^{-1}$, {numref}`tab:dustfix`; {devlog}`D-260`, {devlog}`H-027`) and at most $1.1\times10^{21}$ cm$^{-2}$ on `pn150k` (crossing at $A_{V}=0.39$, $2.79\times10^{21}$ cm$^{-2}$ mag$^{-1}$), part of it molecular. The damping wings of Ly$\beta$, Ly$\gamma$ and Ly$\delta$ alone cover about 4, 1.4 and 0.7 Å of the 912–1110 Å band at $N(\mathrm{H\,I})=10^{21}$ cm$^{-2}$, growing as $N^{1/2}$ (an estimate, {ref}`sec:overlap`). Unmeasured.

6.  **No cross-pumping and no overlap in trapping** ({devlog}`H-006`): a photon absorbed in another line’s wing is lost rather than pumping that line, and the escape of each line ignores the others.

7.  **Electrons are not colliders** of the H$_2$ levels, and the level model has no destruction other than Solomon ({src}`h2_mod.f90` header); this matters in the partly ionised gas, where H$_2$ is already 14–230 times Cloudy’s for chemical reasons ({lim}`L-43`).

8.  **C I photoionisation without H$_2$-line shielding** (`DEVLOG.md`, item 8 of the audit list near line 28742): MOCASSIN’s rate replaces `CIPDRATE`, and MOCASSIN does not transport LW lines, so the rate is overestimated behind the H/H$_2$ transition; estimated at a few tens of per cent at $A_{V}\sim3$, with the opposite sign to the deep C$^+$ deficit ({lim}`L-3`).

9.  **Chlorine is absent from the network**; in Cloudy it carries 25–50% of the H$_2$ sink from $A_{V}=0.26$ on in the PN model, and Cloudy without chlorine turns molecular 0.042 mag earlier ({lim}`L-46`, {devlog}`H-026`). Its absence moves the model’s crossing shallower, by about 0.04 mag on `pn150k`, partly offsetting there the 0.10 mag of item 4 ({lim}`L-46`).

10. **Silicate formation efficiency**: the per-material Cazaux & Tielens surface energies of 3D-PDR give 0.50–0.91 of Cloudy’s coefficient on `hiipdr` ({lim}`L-33`, {devlog}`H-022`); reported, not tuned.

11. **The mean grain temperature could switch formation off** ({devlog}`H-035`; closed by {devlog}`D-414`): the number-weighted mean over the whole size grid counted the bins outside a species' size range at zero temperature; on a grid wider than the grains present this put $T_d$ at $10^{-6}$ K and no H$_2$ formed. Since {devlog}`D-414` the mean is over the bins that hold grains ({ref}`sec:Td`).

12. **Diffuse-site weights** recover 52–130% of the MC’s diffuse field ({lim}`L-4`); the magnitude is the MC’s, the directional distribution, and so the columns assigned to the diffuse light, is approximate ({ref}`sec:sites`).

13. **Unresolved fronts** ({lim}`L-37`, {devlog}`D-088`): on uniform grids the ionisation front and the H/H$_2$ transition are thinner than a cell; the front cells, which carry the warm partly ionised gas through their whole volume, make 92% of `hiipdr`’s \[O I\] 63 μm, 9.3–9.5 times Cloudy’s at $31^3$ and 5.5 times at $61^3$. Front-sensitive lines need a resolution check; a sub-cell estimate of \[O I\] 6300 Å is available offline (`frontLines`).

14. **The gas re-emits from the hydrogen and helium spectrum whatever absorbed** ({lim}`L-51`). A packet absorbed by neutral carbon in the PDR is re-emitted, some of the time, as a hydrogen- or helium-ionising photon: 1.9% of `hiipdr`’s cells per iteration at $A_{V}=2.15$–2.43 are over-ionised, and their chemistry, solved to steady state on that iteration’s ionisation, can lose more than half its H$_2$ (202 such drops in six iterations at the default packet count, 23 at four times, none at sixteen times).

15. **No PAHs or very small grains** ({lim}`L-7`): the photoelectric heating lacks the component that dominates in real PDRs, so the gas is colder than in PDR models with that component at the same field; common-mode with the Cloudy references.

16. **Gas–grain collisional coupling** ({lim}`L-9`, closed by {devlog}`D-420` and {devlog}`D-421`): before v2.0 H$_2$ was not a collider in the exchange, and the term entered the energy balances $4\pi$ too weak (the gas side) and 4 times too weak (the grain side). H$_2$ now collides with the grains with the accommodation $2mM/(m+M)^2$ at $m=m_{\mathrm{H_2}}$, and each balance receives the term in its own units ({ref}`sec:thermal`).

17. **The H$_2$ line emission is computed optically thin and without dust extinction** in `lineFluxH2.out` ({src}`h2Lines_mod.f90` header).

## Found while the H$_2$ report was prepared

18. **Formation flux in the level model is tied to the Solomon flux.** `solveCell` calls `h2Solve` without a sink or a source ({src}`pdr_mod.f90::h2LevelModel/solveCell@"call h2Solve(T, dens, eta, phi, pops, kdiss, pump, beta=beta, heatDis=hDis)"`), so the rate at which new molecules enter the levels in the formation distribution $\phi$ equals the Solomon dissociation rate. The chemistry’s actual formation rate, $R_{\mathrm f}n_{\mathrm H}n(\mathrm H)$, balances all destruction (cosmic rays, ion–molecule reactions, Solomon) in steady state. Where the Solomon rate has been shielded away, formation pumping is therefore underestimated. With the network’s H$_2$ + CRP channels alone the other destruction is $0.99\,\zeta$, $10^{-16}$ s$^{-1}$ at the default `pdrZeta`, so this happens where the Solomon rate is shielded below a few $10^{-16}$ s$^{-1}$ ({ref}`sec:balance`). The energy budget is split the same way: the internal energy that newly formed molecules bring to the levels (and that the collisional term returns to the gas) follows the Solomon flux, while the kinetic part of the formation energy (`heat(5)`) follows the chemistry’s formation rate. Both numbers needed for a consistent closure are already available in `solveCell` ($w_{\mathrm{sil}}+w_{\mathrm{gra}}$, $n(\mathrm H)$, $n(\mathrm{H_2})$): passing the non-Solomon destruction as `sink`, or the chemistry’s formation flux per molecule as `src`, would make the two agree. A per-cell print of formation flux over Solomon flux would size the effect first.

19. **The final output mixes two Solomon rates.** The last `h2LevelModel(grid,12)` ({src}`iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"! runs on the stages that are written."`, {src}`iteration_mod.f90::MCIterationDriver/iterateMC@"if (lgPDR) call h2LevelModel(grid(1:nGrids), 12) ! H-007, H-012"`) runs after the last chemistry, which is not re-solved. The written level populations and $k_{\mathrm{Sol}}$ are those of up to twelve sweeps; the written $n(\mathrm{H_2})$ was obtained with the previous one-sweep rate. The size of the difference is printed by the call itself (“Solomon rate against the last call’s”).

20. **The H$_2$ collisional heating term is frozen and two iterations old in the temperature search.** $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{coll}}$ changes sign and depends steeply on $T$ (it is H$_2$’s line cooling where the gas is warmer than the levels), but it enters `pdrHeatXtra`, which MOCASSIN holds constant in `iterateT`, and the value used to find $T_e^{(n)}$ was computed by the level model at $T_e^{(n-2)}$ ({ref}`sec:lags`). {devlog}`H-030` associates this term, together with the damping, with the two derailed iterations at the start of the chemistry. Tabulating it against $T$, as the CO cooling is ({devlog}`D-215`), would remove the freeze.

21. **Generic photoreactions keep the Draine spectral shape** ({ref}`sec:generic`). {lim}`L-24` corrected the LW-window reactions; the other 24 photoreactions still multiply the 6–13.6 eV band strength by coefficients computed for the Draine shape, although the MC field’s shape changes strongly with depth (its LW share falls from 0.30 to 0.008 on `hiipdr`, {devlog}`D-349`). Reactions whose thresholds lie in the window are overestimated at depth, those driven by the 6–11 eV part underestimated. Folding each reaction’s cross section, or a few sub-bands, over the MC field would remove the assumption.

22. **The level model’s H$_2$ transmission is not shared.** The per-line H$_2$ shielding computed in `solveCell` is exactly what is missing from MOCASSIN’s C I rate (item 8) and what CO’s van Dishoeck & Black factor approximates for H$_2$ screening; neither uses it.

23. **CO shielding and the chemistry’s own H$_2$ diagnostic** exclude the target cell’s own column ({devlog}`D-088`); only the level model averages through it. On `hiipdr`’s grid half a cell can hold $10^{16}$ cm$^{-2}$ of CO, so CO is under-shielded in the cells of its own transition and the transition is placed up to about half a cell too deep ({ref}`sec:avradial`).

24. **Several sources are combined by geometry alone.** The effective $A_{V}$, the source-ward columns, the in-cell chord (`computeAVradial`, {src}`pdr_mod.f90::computeAVradial@"fS = 1.d0/(seg*seg) ! geometric dilution only"`) and the level model’s source-ward level columns (`starColumns`, {src}`pdr_mod.f90::h2LevelModel/starColumns@"fS = 1.d0/(seg*seg)"`) weight the sources by $r_s^{-2}$, without their luminosities or their attenuation, and apply the mean column to the summed direct field through non-linear shielding functions. Exact for one source, which is every fixture; untested for several. Weighting each source by its own attenuated direct field, $L_{\mathrm{FUV},s}r_s^{-2}e^{-R_{\mathrm{FUV}}\tau_{V,s}}$, and evaluating the shielding per source would remove it.

25. **$R_{\mathrm{FUV}}$ is weighted by the emitted spectrum of source 1** ({src}`pdr_mod.f90::dustPerCell@"if (ifreq > 1) then"..@"wsum = wsum + wt(ifreq)"`). It overstates the band extinction of the local, reddened field at depth, and so the optical depth between the scattering sites and the target; this suppresses distant sites increasingly with depth and is a candidate for the fall with depth of the formal solution’s recovery in {lim}`L-4`.

26. **The grain temperature is a number-weighted mean applied to an area-weighted rate** ({ref}`sec:Td`). The mean favours the smallest grains and one efficiency $\epsilon(\langle T_d\rangle)$ replaces $\langle\epsilon(T_d)\rangle$ over the sizes. On silicate at 20–50 K, where $\epsilon$ falls by a factor of 3.6 ({ref}`sec:Td`), the formation rate depends on how the temperatures of the sizes are spread. Evaluating the rate per size with $\pi a^2$ weights would remove both; a temperature per grain material is registered for future development as {devlog}`D-417` ({lim}`L-54`).

27. **No interstellar field at the outer boundary** ({ref}`sec:assumptions`, item 6). On `hiipdr` the source’s field is below $\chi=1$ in the outer 40% of the radius, so the outer PDR is more molecular and colder than the same cloud in the interstellar field.

28. **The coolant line escape depends weakly on the sweep order.** The optical depths of the coolant lines read the other cells’ level populations as stored on the rank ({src}`pdr_mod.f90::pdrStep@"if (lrCell(kray,ipt) /= cellP) evpopR(kray,ipt,1:nlev) = &"`), which cells solved earlier on the same rank have already updated ({src}`pdr_mod.f90::pdrStep@"if (iTpass == iTnear) then"`); the shielding columns are snapshots and do not have this dependence.

29. **The seed double-attenuates.** `seedMolecules` multiplies the local, already attenuated $\chi$ by `SCATTER`$(A_{V})$ ({src}`pdr_mod.f90::seedMolecules@"Dh2 = alphaH2*dble(grid(iG)%chiFUV(cP)) &"`; {ref}`sec:seed`). It affects only the starting point of the first PDR iteration.

30. **The plane-parallel branch includes the near half of the cell in its columns** ({src}`pdr_mod.f90::computeAVdust@"if (cellP > 0) then"..@"grid(iG)%pdrColCO(cellP) = real(cCO)"`), against {devlog}`D-088`’s rule in the point-source branch. The level model does not run in that geometry.

31. **Collisional ionisation of hydrogen lacks the electron density.** MOCASSIN adds $2.75\times10^{-16}T^{3/2}(157\,894/T+2)e^{-157\,894/T}$, a rate coefficient in cm$^3$ s$^{-1}$, to the photoionisation rate in s$^{-1}$ without the factor $n_e$, in its ionisation balance ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/ionBalance@"ionUp(elem,ion) = dble(nPhotoSte(elem,ion))+&"+1`) and in the rate handed to the chemistry ({src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/ionBalance@"grid%pdrPhotRate(cellP, iSl358) = nPhotoSte(elem,1) + collIon"`). Collisional ionisation is therefore misscaled by a factor $n_e$ (underestimated wherever $n_e>1$ cm$^{-3}$); with the factor, the term would reach 10% of a photoionisation rate of $10^{-8}$ s$^{-1}$ at about $1.5\times10^4$ K for $n_e=10^4$ cm$^{-3}$, so hot dense ionised gas is affected and the PDR is not. Inherited from MOCASSIN ({ref}`sec:linked`).

32. **Two reactions of the rate file are inactive.** The grain-assisted recombinations of He$^+$ and C$^+$ (reactions 330 and 331) have no term in {src}`pdr/odes_reduced.c`; their coefficients are computed but unused ({ref}`sec:network`).

33. **H + H$_2$ rates below 100 K** are those of 100 K, the first temperature of the {cite:t}`Lique2015` table, which overestimates the reactive ortho–para conversion by H in cold gas, where H$^+$ exchange usually dominates it ({ref}`sec:coll`).

34. **The H$_2$ line profile is normalised to its own centre** ($\tau_0H(a,x)/H(a,0)$ with Doppler-core $\tau_0$), overstating the Voigt optical depth by $1/H(a,0)$, at most 3% for these data ({ref}`sec:shield`).

35. **Stale or inaccurate comments.** {src}`constants_mod.f90@"! The far-UV band conventionally used for PDR photorates, 912-2400 Angstrom,"` labels the FUV lower edge 2400 Å (the value is 6.0 eV, 2068 Å); the comment in `addOpacity` ({src}`ionization_mod.f90::addOpacity@"! sigma(nu): threshold ~6e-18 cm^2 rising to a broad peak of order"..@"! this failure mode."`) describes a power-law H$_2$ cross section, whereas `yanH2xSec` is the piecewise fit of {cite:t}`Yan1998`; and the comment of `draineWindowShare` ({src}`pdr_mod.f90@"! the window, not a new number. Computed once; D-349 found 0.1516 for"`), {devlog}`D-349` and {lim}`L-24` give the Draine field’s LW share of the continuous band as 0.1516 (0.152), whereas Equation ({eq}`eq:draine`) gives 0.148. The code uses the binned value (0.151–0.164 on the fixtures), consistently with the MC sums, so no result is affected. The comment of `h2FormDist` ({src}`h2_mod.f90@"! distribution of their eqs 4-5 about E_vm = X_vib D, the rotational one the"`) gives the vibrational distribution as “their eqs 4-5” about $E_{vm}=X_{\mathrm{vib}}D$ and the rotational one as “their eqs 6 and 7”, whereas in {cite:t}`TakahashiUehara2001` the vibrational distribution is eqs. 5–7 (with $E_{vm}=X_{\mathrm{vib}}D+E_{\mathrm{off}}$) and the rotational models are eqs. 12 and 13 (the code is right); and {src}`update_mod.f90::updateCell/ionBalance@"! Drake & Ulrich, ApJS42(1980)351"` attributes the collisional-ionisation formula to {cite:t}`DrakeUlrich1980`, which does not contain it ({ref}`sec:linked`).
