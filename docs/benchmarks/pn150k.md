# pn150k: a planetary nebula and its PDR

`pn150k` is a planetary nebula with a thin PDR:
- a 150 kK blackbody of $L=3.83\times10^{36}$ erg s⁻¹ inside a uniform sphere of
  $n_{\mathrm H}=10^3$ cm⁻³;
- inner radius $10^{16}$ cm, outer radius $1.87\times10^{18}$ cm;
- MRN graphite grains of 0.005–0.25 μm at a dust-to-gas mass ratio of 0.0025;
- GASS abundances, with the trace elements set to zero;
- $\zeta=4.3\times10^{-16}$ s⁻¹ in the UMIST normalisation ($2.0\times10^{-16}$ s⁻¹ per H atom,
  Cloudy's cosmic-ray background).

MOCASSIN-PDR computes one octant on $41^3$ cells, 35,384 of them active. The model is
optically thick to the ionising radiation, but its outer edge is only $A_V=0.66$ from the
star, so the H/H₂ transition lies inside it and the C⁺/C transition does not.

The fixture is `benchmarks/pdr/pn150k`. The run shown is the release run `rel-pn41`
({doc}`index`). The code has changed this model since that release, most of all its transition
zone; {ref}`sec:bench-since` gives by how much. Three of its cells are flagged and excluded, at $A_V$ 0.163, 0.240 and
0.273.

## Results at a glance

| Quantity | MOCASSIN-PDR | Cloudy c25.00 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ionisation front (H⁺ = H), $A_V$ / radius | 0.133 / 0.1294 pc | 0.141 / 0.1280 pc |
| H/H₂ transition (H = 2H₂), $A_V$ / radius | 0.393 / 0.364 pc | 0.362 / 0.323 pc |
| Hβ luminosity | 2.69×10³⁴ erg s⁻¹ | 2.80×10³⁴ erg s⁻¹ |

Cloudy's model is a static sphere ({devlog}`D-458`). Its default, expanding sphere let the
nebula's Ly$\alpha$ out through the central hole and into the PDR, where it doubled the
far-ultraviolet field and warmed the gas; the comparisons below give its numbers where they
differ.

In the ionised nebula:
- Hβ (0.96 of Cloudy's) and the front radius (+1.1%) agree;
- of the high-ionisation lines, [O III] 88 μm and [Ar III] 7135 Å are within 1% of Cloudy's,
  [S IV] 10.5 μm, [S III] 18.7 μm and [O IV] 25.9 μm within 7%, and [O III] 5007 Å is 9% high.
  Against the expanding sphere all six were within 5%: as a static sphere Cloudy's own
  [O III] 5007 and 4363 Å are 4% and 7% weaker;
- the low-ionisation lines are high: [O II] 3726, 3729 Å +15–21%, [N II] 6584 Å and
  122 μm +13–25%, C II] 2326 Å +36%;
- [O III] 4363/5007 is 13% high, so the O²⁺ gas is about 5% hotter, and He I 5876 Å is
  +9%;
- the [S II] 6716/6731 ratio is 0.99 against Cloudy's 0.89, which puts MOCASSIN's S⁺
  emission in lower-density gas, as in the front cells ({lim}`L-37`);
- He II 4686 Å is 24% high although MOCASSIN's He²⁺ zone is smaller than Cloudy's and
  6–10% cooler; this is not yet explained.

Behind the front ($A_V$ 0.14–0.20) MOCASSIN's $x(\mathrm H^+)$ is 0.24–0.48 of Cloudy's
and $n_e/n_\mathrm{H}$ 0.39–0.52. MOCASSIN treats the whole photoelectron energy as heat,
with no secondary ionisation by fast photoelectrons, which Cloudy includes; with a
150 kK star this is a likely contributor, not yet measured.

The H/H₂ transition is 0.031 mag deeper than Cloudy's. {devlog}`H-026` took apart the 0.025 mag
by which it was deeper than the expanding sphere's: +0.099 mag from the damping width of the H₂
pumping lines, −0.042 mag from the missing chlorine chemistry ({lim}`L-46`) and −0.031 mag from
the rest. The static sphere's crossing is 0.005 mag shallower ({devlog}`D-458`).

## Reading the comparison

- **The highest stages.** He II 4686 Å is 1.24 times Cloudy's, and [Ne V] 24.3 μm 0.81
  times. Both come from the He²⁺ zone and the hardest part of the stellar spectrum.
- **Neon.** [Ne III] is 1.45–1.47 times Cloudy's in both its optical and infrared lines,
  and [Ne II] 12.8 μm is 1.36 times. The optical and infrared [Ne III] lines differ in
  their temperature sensitivity yet are high by the same factor, so the difference is in
  the Ne²⁺ fraction rather than in the temperature.
- **Low-ionisation and neutral lines.**
  - [O I] 63 μm, 145 μm and 6300 Å are 1.52–1.78 times Cloudy's. As in {doc}`hiipdr`,
    the cells the ionisation front crosses contribute to them ({lim}`L-37`). The
    far-infrared pair was 1.49 and 1.60 against the expanding sphere, whose warmer PDR
    made Cloudy's [O I] 63 μm 19% stronger.
  - [S II] 6716 and 6731 Å are 0.87 and 0.79 of Cloudy's.
- **Carbon in the PDR.**
  - [C II] 158 μm is 0.98 of Cloudy's.
  - [C I] 609 μm and 370 μm are 0.59 and 0.53 of Cloudy's.
  - CO 2–1 is 0.82 of Cloudy's.

  These lines depend on the PDR's temperature:
  - at $A_V=0.22$ it is 1.10 of Cloudy's;
  - from $A_V=0.24$ to the edge it is 0.86–0.97 (the temperature figure above).

  Against the expanding sphere the temperature was 0.83 and 0.67–0.77 of Cloudy's, and
  [C II] 158 μm 0.58: that sphere's PDR took the nebula's Ly$\alpha$, and its far-ultraviolet
  field there is twice the static sphere's ({devlog}`D-458`). The photoelectric efficiency
  per H per unit field was 0.66–1.10 of Cloudy's with no trend ({devlog}`D-304`), but that
  predates the electron-sticking change of {devlog}`D-326`, which lowered MOCASSIN's
  photoelectric heating at a fixed charging parameter by 7–39% on `hiipdr` ({devlog}`D-328`);
  it has not been re-measured here. Against the static sphere the two codes' far-ultraviolet fields
  agree, 0.86–1.03 at $A_V$ 0.20–0.65, while MOCASSIN's total field is 1.14–1.27 times Cloudy's,
  in the optical and the dust infrared ({lim}`L-19`, {devlog}`D-460`).
- **H₂ lines.** Of Cloudy's 40 strongest H₂ lines:
  - the ortho lines are 1.01–1.62 of Cloudy's;
  - the para lines are 1.79–2.67.

  As a static sphere, with its cooler PDR, Cloudy's ortho lines are 0.70–0.89 of the expanding
  sphere's (1-0 S(1) 0.75) and its para lines 0.93–1.07 ({devlog}`D-458`); against the
  expanding sphere the ortho lines were 0.89–1.16 of Cloudy's and the para lines 1.65–2.69.

  The level model damps each pumping line by its upper level's total decay rate; Cloudy
  damps it by the line's own A value, about 20 times smaller in the median, so Cloudy's
  lines absorb less in their wings and its H₂ is pumped and dissociated less
  ({lim}`L-56`, {devlog}`H-005`). Run with Cloudy's width ({devlog}`H-026`, against the
  expanding sphere), the para lines fall to 0.70–0.93 and the ortho lines to 0.45–0.69 of Cloudy's: the width
  accounts for most of the para excess, but a factor 1.3–1.6 between the spins remains,
  attributed to the colder shielded gas ({devlog}`H-009`), and every line then falls
  below Cloudy's.
