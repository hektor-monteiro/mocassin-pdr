# pn150k: a planetary nebula and its PDR

`pn150k` is a planetary nebula with a thin PDR:
- a 150 kK blackbody of $L=3.83\times10^{36}$ erg s⁻¹ inside a uniform sphere of
  $n_{\mathrm H}=10^3$ cm⁻³;
- inner radius $10^{16}$ cm, outer radius $1.87\times10^{18}$ cm;
- MRN graphite grains of 0.005–0.25 μm at a dust-to-gas mass ratio of 0.0025;
- GASS abundances, with the trace elements set to zero;
- $\zeta=4.3\times10^{-16}$ s⁻¹ in the UMIST normalisation ($2.0\times10^{-16}$ s⁻¹ per H atom,
  Cloudy's cosmic-ray background).

MOCASSIN-PDR computes one octant on $41^3$ cells, 35,384 of them active. The model is
optically thick to the ionising radiation, but its outer edge is only $A_V=0.66$ from the
star, so the H/H₂ transition lies inside it and the C⁺/C transition does not.

The fixture is `benchmarks/pdr/pn150k`. The run shown is the release run `rel-pn41`
({doc}`index`). Three of its cells are flagged and excluded, at $A_V$ 0.163, 0.240 and
0.273.

## Results at a glance

| Quantity | MOCASSIN-PDR | Cloudy c25.00 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ionisation front (H⁺ = H), $A_V$ / radius | 0.133 / 0.1294 pc | 0.141 / 0.1279 pc |
| H/H₂ transition (H = 2H₂), $A_V$ / radius | 0.393 / 0.364 pc | 0.368 / 0.328 pc |
| Hβ luminosity | 2.69×10³⁴ erg s⁻¹ | 2.78×10³⁴ erg s⁻¹ |

In the ionised nebula:
- Hβ (0.97 of Cloudy's), the front radius (+1.2%) and the high-ionisation lines agree:
  [O III] 5007 Å and 88 μm, [O IV] 25.9 μm, [S IV] 10.5 μm, [S III] 18.7 μm and
  [Ar III] 7135 Å are within 5% of Cloudy's;
- the low-ionisation lines are high: [O II] 3726, 3729 Å +13–18%, [N II] 6584 Å and
  122 μm +10–23%, C II] 2326 Å +32%;
- [O III] 4363/5007 is 10% high, so the O²⁺ gas is about 4% hotter, and He I 5876 Å is
  +11%;
- the [S II] 6716/6731 ratio is 0.99 against Cloudy's 0.89, which puts MOCASSIN's S⁺
  emission in lower-density gas, as in the front cells ({lim}`L-37`);
- He II 4686 Å is 24% high although MOCASSIN's He²⁺ zone is smaller than Cloudy's and
  4–10% cooler; this is not yet explained.

Behind the front ($A_V$ 0.14–0.20) MOCASSIN's $x(\mathrm H^+)$ is 0.21–0.48 of Cloudy's
and $n_e/n_\mathrm{H}$ 0.36–0.52. MOCASSIN treats the whole photoelectron energy as heat,
with no secondary ionisation by fast photoelectrons, which Cloudy includes; with a
150 kK star this is a likely contributor, not yet measured.

The H/H₂ transition is 0.025 mag deeper than Cloudy's: +0.099 mag from the damping width of the H₂ pumping lines, −0.042 mag from the missing chlorine chemistry ({lim}`L-46`) and −0.031 mag from the rest ({devlog}`H-026`).

## Reading the comparison

- **The highest stages.** He II 4686 Å is 1.24 times Cloudy's, and [Ne V] 24.3 μm 0.85
  times. Both come from the He²⁺ zone and the hardest part of the stellar spectrum.
- **Neon.** [Ne III] is 1.42–1.43 times Cloudy's in both its optical and infrared lines,
  and [Ne II] 12.8 μm is 1.32 times. The optical and infrared [Ne III] lines differ in
  their temperature sensitivity yet are high by the same factor, so the difference is in
  the Ne²⁺ fraction rather than in the temperature.
- **Low-ionisation and neutral lines.**
  - [O I] 63 μm, 145 μm and 6300 Å are 1.49–1.60 times Cloudy's. As in {doc}`hiipdr`,
    the cells the ionisation front crosses contribute to them ({lim}`L-37`).
  - [S II] 6716 and 6731 Å are 0.84 and 0.76 of Cloudy's.
- **Carbon in the PDR.**
  - [C II] 158 μm is 0.58 of Cloudy's.
  - [C I] 609 μm and 370 μm are 0.65 and 0.45 of Cloudy's.
  - CO 2–1 is 0.63 of Cloudy's.

  These lines depend on the PDR's temperature:
  - at $A_V=0.22$ it is 0.83 of Cloudy's;
  - from $A_V=0.24$ to the edge it is 0.67–0.77 (the temperature figure above).

  The photoelectric efficiency per H per unit field was 0.66–1.10 of Cloudy's with no
  trend ({devlog}`D-304`), but that predates the electron-sticking change of
  {devlog}`D-326`, which lowered MOCASSIN's photoelectric heating at a fixed charging
  parameter by 7–39% on `hiipdr` ({devlog}`D-328`); it has not been re-measured here. The
  fields also differ: at $A_V=0.3$ Cloudy carries 0.255 of its local field in the FUV
  against MOCASSIN's 0.094 ({lim}`L-19`).
- **H₂ lines.** Of Cloudy's 40 strongest H₂ lines:
  - the ortho lines are 0.89–1.16 of Cloudy's;
  - the para lines are 1.65–2.69.

  The level model damps each pumping line by its upper level's total decay rate; Cloudy
  damps it by the line's own A value, about 20 times smaller in the median, so Cloudy's
  lines absorb less in their wings and its H₂ is pumped and dissociated less
  ({lim}`L-56`, {devlog}`H-005`). Run with Cloudy's width ({devlog}`H-026`), the para
  lines fall to 0.70–0.93 and the ortho lines to 0.45–0.69 of Cloudy's: the width
  accounts for most of the para excess, but a factor 1.3–1.6 between the spins remains,
  attributed to the colder shielded gas ({devlog}`H-009`), and every line then falls
  below Cloudy's.
